# Virtual Physics Lab

### A Community Engagement Project for Improving Access to Practical Physics Learning

An interactive web-based Virtual Physics Laboratory designed to provide accessible, experiment-based physics learning for school students, particularly where access to physical laboratory equipment is limited.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

Physics is an experimental science, but students may face limitations such as restricted laboratory hours, limited apparatus, equipment maintenance requirements, and high student-to-apparatus ratios.

The **Virtual Physics Lab** provides a digital environment where students can explore selected physics experiments, change experimental parameters, record observations, perform calculations, analyze graphs, and strengthen their understanding through quizzes and viva preparation.

The platform is designed as an **educational prototype and supplementary learning resource**, not as a replacement for physical laboratory work.

## 📊 Data Analysis

The laboratory modules demonstrate how experimental observations can be converted into meaningful physical results.

Examples include:

- **Ohm's Law:** V–I relationship and resistance from graph slope
- **Simple Pendulum:** T²–L relationship and determination of g
- **Hooke's Law:** F–x relationship and determination of spring constant
- **Projectile Motion:** Range variation with launch angle
- **Convex Lens:** 1/v versus 1/u relationship and determination of focal length

The simulations use mathematical models based on standard physics equations.

---

## 🎓 Educational Objectives

The project aims to help students:

1. Understand the physical principles behind experiments.
2. Explore how changing variables affects experimental outcomes.
3. Record and organize observations.
4. Perform physics-based calculations.
5. Interpret graphs and experimental relationships.
6. Identify common experimental errors.
7. Prepare for practical examinations and viva-voce questions.
8. Develop scientific reasoning through repeated experimentation.

---

## 🔄 Learning Workflow

The platform follows a structured learning pathway:

**Learn → Explore → Experiment → Record → Analyze → Understand**

Students can first study the theory, explore the virtual apparatus, perform an experiment, record observations, analyze the resulting data, and review the outcome.
